Draft an entry from a filing

Paste the advisors section of a press release, or a chunk of a first day declaration. Claude reads it and drafts the working group list. The draft appears below for you to check. It is not saved to the site.

Entries are drafted from court docket feeds and public announcements, then checked by a person before they are published. Blank cells mean the advisor was not named in the sources checked, not that the group has no advisor. Verify against the docket before relying on any entry.
